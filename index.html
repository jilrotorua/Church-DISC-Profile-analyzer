Church DISC questionnaire

Answer five scenarios in each section, for 15 answers total.

How scoring works: In each section, a style gets +1 when chosen MOST like you and −1 when chosen LEAST like you. The five answers create a raw tally from −5 to +5. The displayed score is that tally × 12 ÷ 5, rounded to a whole number, so it fits the graph’s −12 to +12 range. This is a simple, custom reflection tool, not a validated assessment.
